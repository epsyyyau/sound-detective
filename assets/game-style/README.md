# Soft Game Artwork

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-10. The approved lab and train previews are the style references. Original artwork is retained in `pictures/`.

## Assets And Prompt Specifications

- `lab-background.png`: 16:9 miniature laboratory, matching the approved preview. Pale yellow wall, mint workbenches, equipment and plants at the far edges. Empty center for interactive bottles and tubes. No game letters, UI or text baked into the image.
- `lab-bottle.png`: One front-facing empty broad laboratory bottle on transparent background, softly dimensional pale aqua glass with ivory highlights. Square canvas. Clear interior for liquid and letters supplied by the game.
- `lab-tube-v2.png`: Restyle the original straight cylindrical tube with matte pale aqua glass, soft ivory highlights and rounded U bottom. Transparent background, no liquid, text, bottle shoulder or cap. Displayed wider for readable letters.
- `bubble-flask.png`: Built-in ImageGen, using the approved group-two gameplay preview as the reference. Extract/recreate only its front-facing round glass flask, coral liquid, and coral-and-cream heater base on a transparent square canvas. Keep the short open neck and soft rounded 3D style. No room, bench, letters, UI, or answer bubbles baked in. Interactive bubbles and sound tiles remain HTML/CSS.
- `bubble-glass-tile.png`: Built-in ImageGen, using the approved gameplay screenshot as a style reference. One blank upright square glass tile, thick rounded pale-aqua bevel, top-left white reflections, milky translucent center, lower/right edge depth and a straight bottom. Transparent square canvas, no letters, stand, room or other objects. Live letters and coral hover/selection frames remain HTML/CSS.
- `bubble-iridescent.png`: Built-in ImageGen, using the same approved screenshot as a style reference. One empty spherical soap bubble, luminous cyan/rose/lilac rim, white curved highlights, pearly reflections, translucent center and a genuinely transparent background. No letters, surrounding bubbles, machine or room. Letters and the correct/wrong interaction remain live HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
- `light-grid-console.png`: Generated on 2026-10-11 from the approved group-four light-grid preview. Empty dimensional green console with two recessed rows, a horizontal divider, bevelled frame, base and feet on a transparent background. No baked-in letters or controls. Live sound pads, scanning light and the answer slot are positioned over this artwork in `light-grid.css`.
- `train-background.png`: 16:9 soft miniature countryside matching the approved train preview. Ivory sky, pale mint and green hills, small rounded trees at the edges, quiet meadow center. No train, track, text, UI or close-up blurry foliage.
- `train-wagon.png`: One blank butter-yellow letter wagon on transparent square canvas. Large ivory inset panel, softly bevelled frame, two brown wheels with ivory hubs and side couplers. Matte miniature clay/paper style. No letter, track or engine.
- `train-tender.png`: One mint coal tender on transparent square canvas, flat side view. Ivory rim, rounded charcoal coal, brown wheels with ivory hubs and right coupling. Match the approved train. No track, letters or other wagons.
- `train-track.png`: One continuous horizontal toy railway on transparent wide canvas. Muted gray rails, pale wood sleepers and rounded light-gray ballast. Match the train miniature material. No train, ground, UI or writing.

All assets use soft daylight, subtle tactile texture, gentle dimensional shading and muted pastel colours. Avoid heavy black cartoon outlines, faces, logos and watermarks.

The engine is reused from `../family-map/train.png`. Group-colour filters affect the wagon illustration; separate unfiltered wheel layers retain the original wheel colours. Letters, liquids, controls and animation remain live HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
