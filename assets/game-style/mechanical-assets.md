# Mechanical Sound-Changing Assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-11.

- `rotary-machine.png`: original pastel-lavender rotary toy with three blank ivory drums, a right-hand crank and a grounded base. Transparent background; no text or interface controls.
- `stamp-machine.png`: original pastel-pink toy press frame, blank paper, brass side rollers and grounded base. Transparent background; no text or moving press head.

The final prompts requested nearly frontal, softly dimensional painted wooden educational toys with clean blank faces for HTML phoneme overlays. The press head is a separate DOM layer so it can align with any selected phoneme and visibly press onto the paper. No original platform vocabulary images were replaced.
